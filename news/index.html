v0.1.0 is ready to download

Crown & Conquest · Nov 12, 2026

Crown & Conquest v0.1.0, the first public Windows build, is on the download page. It is a skirmish against the AI. Play as the Vale Wardens or the Rime Clans. Villagers gather food, wood, gold, and stone, a Homestead raises the population cap, and the match starts in the Seed Age. Pikes, archers, and cavalry counter one another. Maps are Small, Medium, and Large. Large is 96 by 96. The zip holds CrownAndConquest.exe. Windows SmartScreen may warn because the build is not signed. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Two hearths cannot share one valley.
