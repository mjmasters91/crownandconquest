Crowns and Ranked

The new home menu holds Battle, your deck, the Crowns road, and a News tab.

Crowns

Online wins climb a trophy road. Thirteen thousand Crowns makes you Champion, with a Top 100 board.

Ranked

Each month has seven leagues: Squire I, Squire II, Squire III, Knight, Baron, Duke, and Sovereign. The Hall of Legends keeps the season.

News and sound

The News tab reads the live build. Music and sound effects play with the match. Card levels and crates stay on the device.
