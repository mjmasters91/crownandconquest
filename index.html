Crown & Conquest

Two hearths cannot share one valley.

A 2D isometric real-time strategy game. Gather food, wood, gold, and stone, raise a base, and meet the other hearth in the field. Play as the Vale Wardens or the Rime Clans.

Download for Windows Version, install, controls

In the valley

Villagers

Tillers gather food, wood, gold, and stone. A Homestead raises the population cap before the clearing fills.

Two ages

A match opens in the Seed Age. One age remains beyond it, and the base you built is what carries you there.

Counters

Pikes, archers, and cavalry answer one another. Bring the wrong arm to a fight and the other hearth will count it.

Skirmish

Face the AI as the Vale Wardens or the Rime Clans. Normal difficulty is the Rime Clans pressing the valley.

Three maps

Small, Medium, and Large. A Large valley is 96 by 96, with forest, water, and room to lose an army.

Your hands

WASD scrolls the map. Left-drag selects. Right-click commands a move, a gather, or an attack.
