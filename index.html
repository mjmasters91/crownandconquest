Crates and trophies

Win the span, and the yard keeps the score. Everything stays on the device.

Trophies

A fallen Crown Spire pays trophies. Lose some if yours falls. Arenas open as the count climbs.

Crates

A win can leave a crate in the yard. Open it when you get back to the hall.

Card levels

Companions level up. The same eight-card deck hits harder as those levels rise. There is no online ladder yet.
