Crown & Conquest

Two hearths cannot share one valley.

An isometric real-time strategy game. Buildings and units are pre-rendered in 3D, with light and shadow. Gather food, wood, gold, and stone, raise a base, and meet the other hearth in the field. Play as the Vale Wardens or the Rime Clans.

Download for Windows Version, install, controls

In the valley

Villagers

Tillers gather food, wood, gold, and stone. A Homestead raises the population cap before the clearing fills.

Two ages

A match opens in the Seed Age. The Banner Age adds the Market, the Forge, and the Crownkeep, which fires arrows.

Counters

Pikes, archers, and cavalry answer one another. Bring the wrong arm to a fight and the other hearth will count it.

Skirmish

The title screen is a living village, with the emblem, Play, Settings, Credits, and Quit, and a news panel from this site.

Three maps

Small, Medium, and Large. A Large valley is 96 by 96, with forest, water, and room to lose an army.

Your hands

WASD scrolls, with an eased edge. Left-drag selects. Right-click commands. N stops an order. Space grabs an idle Tiller, and Esc pauses.

Multiplayer

Play now splits into Single player and Multiplayer. Host or join a valley over the internet. No port forwarding.

Two to four players. Empty seats can be filled by the AI. Sign in on the account screen, or play as a guest. A signed-in username is remembered and filled in for the lobby. In the ready room, pick your people and talk before the match. In the valley, Enter opens chat.

This is early. If you drop, you cannot rejoin, and your seat is handed to the AI. A desync is reported. It is not repaired.
