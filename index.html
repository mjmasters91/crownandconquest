Crown & Conquest

A portrait real-time 1v1 lane card battler on the river Calderun.

Drag companion cards onto your side of the river, spend elixir, and destroy the enemy Crown Spire. Eighteen original cards. Four arenas. A deck you build yourself. The opponent is an AI, and the save stays on your device.

Sizes, checksums, and install steps

On the span

Your side of the river

Cards enter on your bank of Calderun. They walk the lane toward the enemy Crown Spire.

Elixir

Each companion costs elixir. The number on the card is the cost, from 2 up to 6.

The Crown Spire

The match ends when a Crown Spire falls. Towers along the lane have to be dealt with on the way.

A deck of eight

Pick eight companions. No duplicates. The average elixir cost is shown on the deck screen.

Levels, crates, trophies

Cards level up. A win pays trophies and can leave a crate in the yard.

Offline

The opponent is an AI with three difficulties. Progress is a local save. There is no online play yet.
